How one liquidation is handled

  1. DetectA Chainlink CRE workflow reads the LiquidationCall.
  2. PriceLido rate, Uniswap TWAP and off-chain prices form a band.
  3. JudgeWas the position healthy across the whole band?
  4. PayThe vault re-derives the amount and pays the provable floor.
  5. ChargeThe operator's bond pays first, then the reserve.
  6. RecoverA claim opens against the liquidator and builder.
